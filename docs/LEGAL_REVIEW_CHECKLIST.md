# MISHWAR Legal Review Checklist

Draft pending professional/legal review before public launch. This file is not a legal opinion and does not confirm regulatory approval.

## Review Areas

- Privacy policy and actual data collection.
- Terms of service and user eligibility.
- Driver relationship/model and operational responsibilities.
- Transport licensing and local operating permissions.
- Payments, wallet, refunds, payouts, commission, and settlement model.
- Data retention for finance, fraud, safety, audit, and legal obligations.
- KYC document handling, reviewer access, and secure storage.
- Customer and driver location disclosure, including any future background location change.
- Safety incident handling and escalation claims.
- Tax, accounting, invoice, and ledger obligations.
- Store listing claims and app privacy/data safety declarations.
- Support, account deletion, and data access request procedures.

## Launch Gate

Public launch should not claim full legal compliance, regulatory approval, 24/7 support, direct emergency integration, nationwide coverage, or live digital payment support unless the underlying contracts, operations, provider credentials, policies, and approvals exist.
