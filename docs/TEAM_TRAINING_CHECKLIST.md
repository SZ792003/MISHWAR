# Team Training Checklist

Training foundation for pilot operations. Do not treat this as a final HR, legal, or regulatory policy.

## Operations

- Pilot scope, hours, and capacity understood.
- Command center process understood.
- Day-0 and Day-1 checklist understood.
- Go/No-Go authority understood.
- Daily review template understood.

## Support

- Ticket categories and priorities understood.
- Account restriction response path understood.
- Escalation to safety, finance, risk, and technical understood.
- Does not expose internal risk notes.

### Support Scripts

- No driver: acknowledge, explain driver availability, suggest retry or support follow-up.
- Location problem: ask user to check GPS and choose point manually if needed.
- Payment pending: explain review/payment processing without promising approval.
- Refund requested: confirm request entered review.
- Account restricted: offer account review ticket without exposing internal reason.
- Safety escalation: prioritize and move to Safety Reviewer immediately.

## Safety

- SOS workflow understood.
- Incident acknowledgement understood.
- Ride/location context review understood.
- No claim of automatic police/ambulance notification.
- Outcome documentation understood.

## Finance

- Cash confirmation policy understood.
- Refund review understood.
- Payout review understood.
- Daily reconciliation understood.
- Manual adjustment ledger/audit requirement understood.

## Driver Onboarding

- Signup/profile/KYC/vehicle review sequence understood.
- Approval checklist understood.
- Training acknowledgement captured.
- First ride support path understood.

## Technical

- Health/ready/version checks understood.
- Deployment smoke test understood.
- Rollback and kill switch process understood.
- Hotfix process understood.
- Incident drill process understood.

## Driver Training Content

- How to go online.
- How to accept a ride.
- GPS and location permission expectations.
- Pickup and arrival.
- Ride start/end.
- Cash confirmation only after receiving cash.
- SOS and safety reporting.
- Support ticket and account restriction review.

## Customer Education Content

- Requesting a ride.
- Selecting pickup/destination.
- Choosing vehicle type.
- Tracking driver.
- Payment method availability.
- SOS use.
- Support ticket and refund request.
