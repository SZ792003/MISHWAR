# MISHWAR Terms of Service Draft

Draft pending professional/legal review before public launch. This is not a final legal opinion or regulatory approval.

## Service Use

MISHWAR provides ride request, driver dispatch, trip status, payment workflow, and operational support features in approved service areas.

## Accounts

Users must provide accurate account information. Drivers may be required to complete KYC and operational approval before receiving ride requests.

## Customer Responsibilities

- Provide accurate pickup and destination details.
- Use the service lawfully and respectfully.
- Pay the fare through enabled payment methods.
- Report safety or payment issues through support channels.

## Driver Responsibilities

- Keep profile and vehicle information accurate.
- Provide truthful KYC and vehicle information.
- Use one account and do not share the account.
- Follow applicable traffic and safety requirements.
- Use the app to update ride status accurately.
- Share location during active ride workflows when required.
- Confirm cash receipt only when payment is actually received.
- Follow the enabled payout/commission framework once it is operationally approved.

## Payments

Payment methods may include cash, wallet, or digital provider options depending on activation. Refunds, payouts, settlements, and adjustments are handled through backend financial controls and may require review.

Digital payment availability depends on region, provider availability, commercial activation, and operational readiness. It should not be described as live until enabled.

## Cancellations and Refunds

Cancellation and refund eligibility must be finalized before launch. Refund requests may be approved, rejected, or manually reviewed according to operational policy.

## Safety

Users and drivers must not misuse the platform. Safety reports, SOS flows, account blocks, and audit logs may be used to protect users and operations.

## Account Suspension

Accounts may be suspended or blocked for safety, fraud, policy, legal, payment, or operational reasons.

## Service Limits

Service availability, vehicle types, cities, payments, and notifications may depend on pilot configuration, Firebase availability, provider availability, and operational coverage.

## Support and Disputes

Support channels:

- SUPPORT_URL
- CONTACT_EMAIL

Disputes and incidents should be submitted through official support channels once published.

## Prohibited Behavior

Users and drivers must not share OTPs, misuse safety tools, create duplicate accounts to avoid review, submit false KYC data, harass others, or attempt payment/refund/payout abuse.
