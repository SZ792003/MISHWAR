# MISHWAR Help Center Draft

Draft pending product, operations, and legal review before public launch.

## Customer

- How do I request a ride? Enter pickup and destination, choose vehicle type and enabled payment method, then submit the request.
- How do I set my location? Use current location only after the location education prompt, or choose the point manually on the map.
- Can I change the destination? Destination changes should be handled through support until an in-trip destination edit flow is formally released.
- What if no driver is available? Retry later, adjust pickup if inaccurate, or contact support if the issue persists.
- How do I cancel a ride? Use the cancel action before the ride reaches a final state.
- How do I report a problem? Create a support ticket from the app and include the ride context when available.
- How do I use SOS? Use SOS only for urgent safety concerns during a ride. It alerts MISHWAR operations; it is not documented as direct government emergency integration.
- What if location is inaccurate? Move to an open area, check device GPS settings, or choose the point manually.
- How does payment work? Available methods depend on region, provider availability, and operational activation. Cash and wallet may be enabled; digital provider payment requires activation.
- How do I request a refund? Use refund/support after payment, subject to operational review.

## Driver

- How do I become available? Complete profile/KYC when required, wait for approval, then go online.
- How do I accept a ride? Review the incoming request and tap accept if you can complete it safely.
- How do I reach the customer? Follow the route and update ride status in the app.
- How do I start and complete a ride? Mark arrived, then start, then complete after the trip ends.
- How do I confirm cash? Confirm cash only after receiving the amount.
- How do I review earnings? Use the earnings and wallet section in the driver app.
- How do I request a payout? Request payout when a balance is available and payouts are operationally enabled.
- Why is my account restricted? Contact support with `account_review`; internal risk reasons are not shown in-app.
- How do I upload/review KYC? Submit required document metadata through the KYC section; documents are private and reviewed by authorized reviewers.
- How do I report an incident? Use SOS for urgent issues or support/safety report for non-urgent issues.

## Payments

Payment methods are activated by region and provider readiness. MISHWAR should not claim live digital payments until provider credentials, contracts, and production testing are complete. The system records ride payments, wallet ledger entries, refunds, payouts, commission, and settlements, but it must not store CVV, bank PIN, OTP, or raw card credentials.

## Safety

Do not share OTPs, financial credentials, or sensitive personal data. Use SOS for urgent ride safety concerns and support tickets for account or payment review. Private safety reports and comments are not public profile content.

## Account

Users can request account deletion or data access. Requests enter manual review because some financial, fraud, safety, audit, or legal records may require retention.

## Technical Issues

If maps, notifications, or payments fail, try again with stable connectivity and send a support ticket with the visible issue. Do not include passwords, OTPs, or raw payment credentials.
